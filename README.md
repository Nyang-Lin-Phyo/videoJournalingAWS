meowd# reverie.

A private video journal for the days you want to remember.

Record a video or upload one, give it a title, and save it. Reverie transcribes it and puts together a short reflection. You can revisit entries on a timeline or calendar, edit journal pages, keep a streak going, and turn your memories into PDFs.

## Built with

React, TypeScript, Vite · AWS (S3, Lambda, SQS, DynamoDB, Cognito, CloudFront) · ElevenLabs · Gemini
